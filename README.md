# Amazon Sales Analysis (Interactive Dashboard creation using Tableau & Excel)

## Project Objective
Amazon wants to analyze its annual sales data for 2022 to gain insights into customer behavior, optimize fulfillment strategies, and enhance sales growth for 2023 & 2024.

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/kalyan-reddy-b/Data-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/Amazon%20Sale%20Report.csv">Amazon Sales Data<a/>

## Questions (KPIs) Addressed
- Comparison of sales and orders using a single chart.
- Identification of the month with the highest sales and orders.
- Analysis of purchase behavior across different categories.
- Overview of different order statuses in 2022.
- Listing of the top 10 states contributing to sales.
- Relationship between order size and fulfillment method.
- Analysis of sales contributions from different fulfillment methods.
- Identification of the highest-selling category.
- Calculation of the percentage of total orders delivered.

## Process
1. **Data Preprocessing & Cleaning**: 
   - Verified data for missing values and anomalies and resolved inconsistencies.
   - Ensured data consistency in terms of type, format, and values.
2. **Excel Dashboard Development**:
   - Processed and cleaned data using Excel functions and formulas.
   - Created pivot tables and charts for interactive analysis.
   - Designed multiple dashboards covering various KPIs.
3. **Tableau Dashboard Development**:
   - Imported cleaned data from Excel into Tableau.
   - Created visualizations in Tableau based on the defined KPIs.
   - Designed an interactive dashboard incorporating key insights.

## Insights from the Analysis
- **Total Sales Revenue:** 78,590,170
- **Total Orders Shipped:** 1,06,571
- **Amazon Fulfillment Rate:** 69.56%
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** 653.7
- **Top Shipping States:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, and Uttar Pradesh.
- **Most Cancelled Orders:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Telangana.
- **Category Performance:** Shirts and T-shirts lead in sales.
- **Fulfillment Methods:** 69.79% of orders are fulfilled by Amazon, while 30.21% are by Merchants.
- **Size Preference:** Medium (M) and Large (L) sizes are the most ordered.

## Final Conclusion
To enhance Amazon's sales performance, a strategic plan should focus on key consumer behaviors, optimizing fulfillment strategies, and improving customer experience. Key recommendations include:
- Implementing personalized marketing campaigns.
- Enhancing inventory management for popular product categories.
- Expanding logistics and fulfillment efficiency.
- Improving customer service and return policies.
- Leveraging both Excel and Tableau dashboards for continuous data-driven decision-making.
